Support

Turning tools on, purchases, and where to ask for help.

Turn a tool on: Mac

  1. Open the Multitool app and choose a tool. Its page shows whether it is on in Safari.
  2. Click Open Safari Settings…, or in Safari choose Safari › Settings › Extensions.
  3. Tick the tool. If it needs access to websites, Safari asks; each tool’s page in the app says what to choose.

Turn a tool on: iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  2. Tap the tool and turn on Allow Extension.
  3. If the tool works on every page you open (Page Unlocker, Text Highlighter, Dark Mode, Stop All Scripts, Markdown Viewer), set All Websites to Allow.

Each tool’s page on this site and in the app has its own steps and a demo page to try it on.

Free and paid tools

Multitool is a free download. JSON Prettifier and Full Page Screenshot are free. Every other tool is unlocked with its own in-app purchase, or together with the Tool Pack, which unlocks every paid tool available on the day you buy it. Tools added later are sold on their own, and in a new pack. Purchases are one-time: no subscription and no account.

You can turn a paid tool on in Safari before you buy it. Until you do, its toolbar button shows what it does and a button to unlock it in the app.

Common questions

I bought a tool on another device

Purchases work on your Mac, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account. Open Multitool and choose Restore Purchases.

A tool says it’s locked after I bought it

Open the Multitool app once, so it can check your purchases, then reload the page in Safari. If it still shows as locked, choose Restore Purchases.

A tool doesn’t appear in Safari

Quit Safari and open it again. On a Mac, if the tool is still missing from Safari’s Extensions settings, open the Multitool app once from your Applications folder.

The toolbar button says it can’t reach this page

Safari hasn’t allowed the tool on this website. Allow it in Safari’s settings for the extension, then reload the page. Safari doesn’t let extensions into its own pages, such as Settings or the Start Page.

Family Sharing

Purchases can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Refunds

The App Store handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact

Questions, a website where a tool doesn’t work, or ideas: macapps.support@icloud.com. Please include the tool, your device, and the page’s address if it is about a website.
