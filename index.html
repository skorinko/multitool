Questions

Why are they separate extensions?

So that each tool asks Safari only for what it needs. JSON Prettifier and Color Picker see a page only when you ask; Page Unlocker and Dark Mode need the websites you visit. You turn on only the tools you use.

Can I try a paid tool before buying it?

Each tool’s page here has screenshots and a demo. Turned on in Safari before you buy it, a paid tool’s toolbar button shows what it does and how to unlock it.

Does the Tool Pack include tools added later?

No. It unlocks every paid tool available on the day you buy it, and those stay yours. New tools are sold on their own, and in a new pack.

Does one purchase cover my Mac, iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Purchases work on every device with the same Apple Account; on a new device, choose Restore Purchases in the app.

What data does it collect?

None. Everything stays in Safari on your device. Read the privacy policy.
