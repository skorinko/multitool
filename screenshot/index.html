Full Page Screenshot Free

The whole web page, top to bottom, in one picture.

Coming soon to the App StoreTry it in Safari

Free with Multitool. Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Full Page Screenshot captures an entire web page, top to bottom, as one picture. Click its button in Safari’s toolbar, or press Control-Shift-S on a Mac, and it scrolls the page for you, joins every screen into one sharp image, and puts the page back where you were reading.

The hard pages, done right

Most full-page tools join screenshots and hope. Full Page Screenshot prepares the page first: sticky headers and cookie bars appear once, where they belong; pictures that load as you scroll are loaded before the capture; and the last screen lands exactly where the page ends, so nothing is cut off.

What it does

  • Sticky headers once, lazy pictures loaded, inner scroll areas included
  • Crop, arrows, shapes, text, highlight and blur
  • PNG, JPEG or PDF, or copy to the clipboard

Turn it on

On a Mac

  1. Open Safari Settings and choose Extensions.
  2. Tick Full Page Screenshot.
  3. On any web page, click the Full Page Screenshot button in the toolbar, or press ⌃⇧S to capture at once.

No website access is needed in advance. Safari lets the extension capture a page only when you click its button.

On iPhone and iPad

Turn it on in Settings

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari.
  2. Tap Extensions, then Full Page Screenshot.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.

Then, on any web page

  1. Tap the page menu button beside the address.
  2. Tap Full Page Screenshot, then Full Page.

No website access is needed in advance. Safari lets the extension capture a page only when you tap it.
