Page Extractor lists everything on a web page that you might want to take with you: every link, every email address and every picture. Click its button in Safari’s toolbar and the counts are there at once. Copy a whole list in one click, or open the full view to filter, pick and export.

Pictures come in their full-size version where there is one, and the ones you pick save as one ZIP with the sites’ own file names. Email addresses that sites try to hide are found too.

Turn it on

On a Mac

  1. Open Safari Settings and choose Extensions.
  2. Tick Page Extractor.
  3. On any web page, click the Page Extractor button in the toolbar, or press ⌘⇧E.

No website access is needed in advance. Safari lets Page Extractor read a page only when you click its button.

On iPhone and iPad

Turn it on in Settings

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari.
  2. Tap Extensions, then Page Extractor.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.

Then, on any web page

  1. Tap the page menu button beside the address.
  2. Tap Page Extractor.

No website access is needed in advance. Safari lets Page Extractor read a page only when you tap it.
