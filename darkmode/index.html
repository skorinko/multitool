Every website in Safari in dark mode, when you want it.

Dark Mode turns every website dark, the moment your Mac, iPhone or iPad is. Pages are dark from the first frame, so there is no white flash while they load, and text is always easy to read.

No white flash

Most dark mode extensions show each page white for a moment before darkening it. Dark Mode has Safari paint a dark background before the page appears, then switches straight to the site’s dark theme, remembered from your last visit.

Photos stay photos

Pictures and videos are never inverted. They are dimmed slightly so they don’t glare, and you choose how much. Small dark icons that would vanish on a dark page are flipped to stay visible.

Turn it on

On a Mac

  1. Open Safari Settings and choose Extensions.
  2. Tick Dark Mode. A welcome page opens where you choose when websites go dark.
  3. Click Edit Websites… and set When visiting other websites to Allow, so every site goes dark without asking. The welcome page has a button for this too.

On iPhone and iPad

Turn it on in Settings

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari.
  2. Tap Extensions, then Dark Mode.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.
  4. Under Permissions, set All Websites to Allow, so every site goes dark without asking.

Or from Safari

  1. Open any website and tap the page menu button beside the address.
  2. Tap Manage Extensions and turn on Dark Mode.
  3. Tap Dark Mode in the same menu, then Always Allow… and Always Allow on Every Website.
