Text Highlighter lets you mark text on any web page in Safari, the way you would with a highlighter pen. Select a sentence, pick a colour, and it stays marked. Come back tomorrow, next week or next year, and your highlights are still there.

Highlights that come back

Web pages change: an ad loads late, a paragraph is added, a word is corrected. Text Highlighter remembers not only where your text was but what it said and what came before and after it, so it finds it again when the page has moved, and even when a few words have changed. If the text is gone, the highlight isn’t thrown away: it’s listed as not found, so you never lose a quote.

What it does

  • Five colours and notes, on any web page
  • Highlights come back each time you open the page
  • Search them all, and export to Markdown, Obsidian or Readwise

Turn it on

On a Mac

  1. Open Safari Settings and choose Extensions.
  2. Tick Text Highlighter, then choose Always Allow on Every Website.
  3. Select text on any web page and pick a colour. Or press ⌃⇧H.

Text Highlighter needs access to the websites you visit so it can show your highlights when you come back. Nothing leaves your Mac.

On iPhone and iPad

Turn it on in Settings

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari.
  2. Tap Extensions, then Text Highlighter.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension, then set All Websites to Allow.

Then, on any web page

  1. Select some text, then tap a colour in the bar under it.
  2. To see a page's highlights, tap the page menu button beside the address, then Text Highlighter.

Text Highlighter needs access to the websites you visit so it can show your highlights when you come back. Nothing leaves your device.
