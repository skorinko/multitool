What it does

  • Pick any pixel with a magnifier, or the exact CSS color of any element
  • See a page's whole palette and its color variables
  • Copy as HEX, OKLCH, Display P3, SwiftUI, Tailwind and more, with contrast checks

Turn it on

On a Mac

  1. Open Safari Settings and choose Extensions.
  2. Tick Color Picker. It needs no access to your websites: it reads a page only when you click its button.
  3. On any web page, click the Color Picker button in the toolbar, or press ⌃⇧C.

Color Picker sees a page only when you ask it to pick a color there. Nothing leaves your Mac.

On iPhone and iPad

Turn it on in Settings

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari.
  2. Tap Extensions, then Color Picker.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.

Then, on any web page

  1. Tap the page menu button beside the address, then Color Picker, then Pick a Color.
  2. Drag your finger over the page and lift it to copy the color under the magnifier.

Color Picker sees a page only when you ask it to pick a color there. Nothing leaves your device.
